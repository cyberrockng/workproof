WorkProof does not ask an admin to decide whether work is acceptable. The job moves through a deterministic escrow lifecycle, and settlement depends on a signed FCC ActionResult.
The client funds principal plus protocol fee and commits to a private bundle hash, ciphertext hash, engine hash, deadlines, and the expected TEE selected before encryption. The named contractor accepts before `acceptBy`. If the job is never accepted, the client can cancel and recover funds after the acceptance window. The contractor submits the deployed contract artifact. The escrow records its runtime codehash at submission so later bytecode changes cannot silently settle. The escrow locks a Flare secure random round. Hidden test-vector selection is derived from that randomness and the committed job data. The Go extension decrypts the private bundle, checks commitments, re-reads chain state, executes selected vectors, and signs a VerdictV1 payload. PASS pays the contractor and treasury. FAIL or INCONCLUSIVE does not pay. Once grace expires, refund remains available to the client.
