Protocol flow

Lock, verify privately, settle on-chain.

The escrow contract pins a specific TEE for each job and only accepts a signed verdict whose fields match the job, attempt, artifact, randomness, and instruction.

Principal and fee are locked in FTestXRP on Coston2 with deadlines and hidden-vector commitment. The artifact address and runtime codehash are recorded before verification can be dispatched. The Go verifier decrypts the private bundle, derives vector selection from Flare randomness, and signs VerdictV1. PASS pays, FAIL keeps funds locked for resubmission, and expiry allows client refund.

Why it matters

Objective acceptance without exposing private tests.

WorkProof targets smart-contract bounties where payment should depend on concrete executable behavior, but revealing all tests upfront lets contractors optimize only against the public suite. The TEE receives encrypted hidden vectors, verifies the deployed artifact, and produces a settlement-grade signature.

Escrow-bound verdicts

Every accepted PASS is bound to chain ID, escrow, job, attempt, instruction ID, artifact, randomness, engine, and report hash.

Failure does not pay

FAIL and INCONCLUSIVE do not release contractor funds; the job moves to resubmission or refund behavior.
