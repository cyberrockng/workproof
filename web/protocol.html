WorkProofEscrow is both the escrow and FCC instruction sender. It pins the TEE at job creation, dispatches VERIFY, and validates a signed VerdictV1 before moving funds.

Lifecycle states

The state machine prevents payment before a submitted artifact has been bound to a random round and verified by the pinned TEE.

Created Accepted Submitted ReadyToVerify Verifying AwaitingResubmission RefundPending Paid Refunded

Outcome table

TEE outcome Contract state Funds movement Operator action
PASS Paid Principal to contractor, fee to treasury Any relayer can submit the signed result
FAIL AwaitingResubmission or RefundPending No contractor payment Contractor may resubmit before deadline
INCONCLUSIVE AwaitingResubmission or RefundPending No contractor payment Retry or refund depending on timing
Expired Refunded Locked amount returns to client Client calls refund after grace

TEE signature model

The escrow recovers the signer from the FCC result and requires it to match the TEE pinned on the job.

Replay protection

Verdicts are bound to the instruction ID, job, attempt, chain, escrow, issue time, and expiry.

Wrong-signer protection

A valid signature from another TEE, extension, or chain cannot settle the job.
