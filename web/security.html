WorkProof is designed so a settlement result must match the escrow's own state. Known limitations are documented directly because this demo is not production software.

Fund safety controls

Only PASS releases payment. FAIL, INCONCLUSIVE, late results, and expired jobs do not pay the contractor.

Artifact binding

The submitted artifact address, block, and runtime codehash are checked again before settlement.

Randomness binding

TEE vector selection depends on a locked Flare random round and committed job data.

Replay protection

Verdicts bind chain ID, escrow, job, attempt, instruction ID, report hash, issue time, and expiry.

Wrong-signer rejection

The recovered signer must be the TEE pinned for that job and registered under the WorkProof extension.

Audit required

A formal third-party audit is required before production or mainnet use with valuable assets.

Known limitations

  • Current deployment uses simulated attestation, not GCP AMD SEV hardware attestation.
  • Verifier RPC quorum is not implemented; production should compare independent providers.
  • Generic proxy artifacts remain a residual risk unless denied or governed by an immutable-artifact policy.
  • Role-separated demo evidence still requires separate client, contractor, and relayer keys.
  • Mainnet deployment requires monitoring, key rotation, verified infra, and external audit signoff.
